How to read this portal

These updates are intentionally simplified and are not medical advice.

  • Repositioning schedule — when your loved one is turned to relieve pressure on the skin.
  • Mattress performance — whether the mattress is working correctly and helping redistribute pressure.
  • Risk status — a simplified traffic-light view (visible to the care team) of skin-breakdown risk.

For any clinical question, please speak directly with the nursing team. This portal does not replace medical guidance.
